Avery 5160 / 8160 Label Maker

Turn a Google, Outlook, or spreadsheet CSV into printable address labels.

How it works

  1. 1 Export contacts as CSV from Google Contacts or Outlook, or download the spreadsheet template and fill it in Google Sheets or Excel.
  2. 2 Choose the matching CSV type and any options you need. Spreadsheet files from the template use Google CSV.
  3. 3 Load the CSV to generate labels. If you edited the template in Sheets or Excel, save or download it as CSV first.
  4. 4 Click × to remove a label, or F to change the name to The LASTNAME Family.
  5. 5 Edit text as needed, then print on letter-size Avery 5160 / 8160 sheets. Use the printer settings below so the labels line up.
Printer setup
  • Set scale to 100% (Actual size). Do not use Fit to page or Fit to printable area.
  • Set margins to Default and turn headers and footers off.
  • Print a test page on plain paper first, then load the label stock the way your printer tray indicates.
